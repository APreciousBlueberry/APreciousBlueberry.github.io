Quick pitch

My edit of the movie Annihilation. This version is more mysterious with less exposition. I cold-open enter the shimmer at just 3 minutes instead of waiting 30 minutes. I reveal backstory gradually through flashbacks, and I cut the college and infidelity subplot. I moved the meteor strike to the final act, preserving the mystery of the shimmer, and I inserted a cosmic sequence from another film, The Tree of Life, to add wonder and suspense to the climax. The result is 25 minutes shorter and more atmospheric.

Details

The biggest change in this edit is that we enter the shimmer almost immediately. In the original film we spend 30 minutes on exposition before finally crossing the boundary, but in this version we cross it at just 3 minutes. With the anomaly ahead and the military behind, the audience can understand the situation without a word of dialogue. After all, even with 30 minutes of exposition we still don't really know what the shimmer is. ("We have many theories, few facts.") So rather than explain everything up front, I instead moved the exposition to later in the film and I reframed it as a flashback.

I also removed the college and infidelity subplot. The infidelity wasn't in the original book, and it feels like the movie added that to give Lena extra motivation for entering the shimmer. But I think the mystery of what happened to her dying husband is already motivation enough, especially since there's no catharsis or resolution to the infidelity storyline. In this edit, Lena's only backstory is that her husband was in a previous expedition, and even that information is revealed gradually rather than explained at the beginning.

Another significant change is I moved the meteor strike from the opening of the film to the end of Act 2. This preserves the mystery about the shimmer's origins for much longer. And I combined the meteor strike with a 4-minute selection from Terrence Malick's 20+ minute cosmic sequence in The Tree of Life, adding a sense of both wonder and suspense as Lena approaches the lighthouse for the film's climax.

And finally I retitled the film to The Shimmer, rather than Annihilation. The original title comes from the book, where "annihilation" is a hypnotic trigger word used by Ventress to compel the team members to commit suicide. That entire mind-control subplot was dropped from the film adaptation, but the title remained, presumably for its connection and brand recognition with the book. But without that storyline, I think The Shimmer is a more fitting title.

The original film runs 1h 55m. This edit runs 1h 30m, removing 25 minutes.

I don't profit from this fan-edit in any way, and this edit should not be used as a substitute for purchasing the original work. To watch this edit, you're expected to already have legal access to the original movie, such as on blu-ray or through a streaming service.
